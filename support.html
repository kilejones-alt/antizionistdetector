Support

Contact

For support, corrections, privacy inquiries, legal inquiries, app-store issues, or ownership questions, contact Kile Jones through the public contact link at kilejones.substack.com. If the app is distributed through an app store, use the public developer contact listed there.

Access and purchases

The GitHub Pages detector includes three completed analyses per browser. Purchase App Now opens the separate Stripe purchase page for the downloadable unlimited app. Owner-authorized users can enter the private access code again on another browser or device.

What to include

For bug reports, include the app version, the page URL, your device/browser, the exact result shown, and a screenshot if possible. For rule corrections, include the exact text tested and what result you expected.

Do not send private material

Do not send confidential, privileged, private, or sensitive text unless you are comfortable sharing it for support review. If possible, replace names or identifying details before sending examples.

Update policy

The app should be updated when new patterns are added, false positives are found, app-store rules change, or privacy/data practices change. New detector logic should be added with test cases before release.

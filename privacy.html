Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

App covered by this policy

This policy applies to The Antizionism Detector, a browser-based text-analysis app by Kile Jones.

Privacy contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to Kile Jones through the public contact link at kilejones.substack.com, or through the public developer contact listed on the app’s Google Play listing if the app is distributed there.

Pasted text and uploaded documents

Pasted text and PDF, DOCX, TXT, JPG, PNG, or WebP contents are analyzed locally in the browser and are not transmitted to the developer. When OCR is needed, the browser downloads the Tesseract.js recognition code and English language model from their hosting services; the selected document or image remains in the browser.

Web-page scanning

When you submit a public web-page or website URL, that public URL is sent to the configured web-reader service (currently Jina Reader) so the page can be retrieved and readable text returned to your browser. Website crawling follows same-site links found in retrieved public pages within the selected page limit. The detector does not intentionally retain submitted URLs or retrieved page text in its own backend because this GitHub Pages build has no detector-analysis backend. The web-reader provider may process ordinary request and diagnostic data under its own policies.

History and corrections saved on your device

Analysis metadata, matched evidence snippets, source labels, corrections, the three-use counter, and private-code unlock status are saved locally in browser storage. Full pasted text is not stored in local history. Pasted text and extracted document text are analyzed locally and are not sent to a detector-analysis server. You can delete local history from the History page and reset site data through the browser.

Protected commercial application

This package is already unlocked. It does not require payment verification, a user account, or an access cookie.

Other data collection by this app

The app does not create public profiles or collect precise location, contacts, photos, health information, or advertising identifiers. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Hosting and ordinary technical logs

GitHub Pages, Stripe, the configured public web-reader service, and external OCR/code hosts may receive ordinary technical request data needed to deliver their services. Those systems are separate from the detector’s local rule engine.

Android / Google Play distribution

The application uses GitHub Pages hosting, Stripe Checkout, external OCR resources, and a public-page retrieval service for URL and website scanning. Those providers may process ordinary technical, payment, delivery, or request data under their own policies. The detector does not include advertising and does not transmit pasted or document-extracted text to an analysis server.

Sharing and sale of data

The detector does not sell personal data. The detector does not share pasted text, uploaded-document contents, submitted URLs, or retrieved website text with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics services. GitHub Pages, the public web-reader service, Stripe, and OCR code/model hosts may process limited network information needed to provide their respective services, secure the service, or diagnose failures.

Security

The public site should be served over HTTPS. Locally saved corrections remain on your device and are protected by your browser, device, and operating-system security settings.

Retention and deletion

Pasted text, uploaded-document contents, and retrieved page text are not retained by the detector after analysis unless you keep the text in the text box or save a local correction. Locally saved analysis metadata and corrections remain on your device until you clear them from the History page or clear the app’s browser storage. Full pasted text is not stored in local history.

Children

The app is not directed to children. It is intended for users who can evaluate political, legal, and historical language in context.

Legal, ownership, and liability notice

Ownership, permitted use, disclaimers, and limitations of liability are addressed on the Legal & ownership page. Privacy concerns what data the app collects, uses, stores, or shares; it is not a full terms-of-use agreement.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app adds accounts, analytics, ads, subscriptions, cloud storage, feedback submission, or any other data practice not described here.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This policy applies to The Antizionism Detector, developed by Kile Jones.

Data used by the app

The app processes content users choose to submit, upload, photograph, or scan—including text, documents, images, URLs, and website content—to provide detector functions. The app may also use browser or device storage for app settings, usage state, history, and corrections.

Service providers and sharing

Hosting, payment, OCR, web-retrieval, browser, and app-distribution providers may process information needed to deliver their respective services. These providers can include GitHub Pages, Stripe, OCR/model hosts, and the configured web-reader service. Information is used or shared only as needed to operate, secure, support, and provide the app and its requested features, or as required by law.

Security

Network communications are served over HTTPS where supported, and reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the app and its service providers.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the feature and service involved. Browser-stored history and corrections can be removed from the History page or by clearing the app or site data. Other retained information is kept only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, security, legal obligations, and product operations.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, providers, or data practices change.

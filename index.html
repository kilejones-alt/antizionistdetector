    How to read results

    No antizionism detected

    The app did not find antizionist language.

    Context-dependent antizionist language

    The app found mapped vocabulary whose attribution, target, or function requires surrounding context.

    Antizionist language found

    The text uses antizionist language directly.

    Strong antizionist language found

    The text uses stronger language, such as libel, Zionism/Nazi inversion, replacement language, exclusion, violence-shielding, or Jewish-institution targeting.

    What this detector checks

    Antizionist language: words, slogans, libels, and chants tied to Israel, Zionism, Zionists, Israelis, the IDF, Jewish statehood, Jewish indigeneity, Jewish institutions, or recognized antizionist vocabulary.

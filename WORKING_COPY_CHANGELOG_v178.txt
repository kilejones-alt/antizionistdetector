CONTROLLING ATTRIBUTION RULE
- A mapped expression counts when the author uses it indicatively in their own voice: affirming, endorsing, agreeing with, accepting, approving, condoning, backing, or otherwise presenting it as correct/true.
- Quoted, reported, rejected, criticized, mocked, descriptive, academic, historical, or attribution-unclear wording does not count as the author’s antizionist language.
- Genuinely unresolved stance remains human-review/unclear rather than forced into endorsement.

GENOCIDE CLARIFICATION
- An affirmative author accusation that Israel/Zionism is committing genocide counts as antizionist language.
- Explicit affirmation of the accusation itself also counts, including formulations such as “the genocide accusation against Israel is correct.”
- Reporting that others make the accusation, quoting it, rejecting it, or discussing it academically/historically does not count.
- Genocide language unrelated to Israel/Zionism is not mapped by this rule.
